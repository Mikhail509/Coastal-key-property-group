Frequently Asked Questions

Questions owners ask.

Do you manage short-term rentals?

Yes, short-term management is one of our planned services. Property eligibility depends on the property's location, building rules and applicable requirements.

Do you manage properties for 30+ day stays?

Yes. We can discuss mid-term and long-term management options depending on the property and owner's goals.

How much does management cost?

Our current website packages are 15%, 20% and 25% of gross booking revenue, depending on the level of service. Final scope and pricing are confirmed after a property assessment.

Do I have to live in South Florida?

No. We can discuss management options with owners who live elsewhere, subject to property eligibility and the agreed service scope.

Do you guarantee a specific amount of rental income?

No. Rental performance varies by property, market conditions, seasonality, building rules and other factors. We can discuss strategy and potential without guaranteeing results.
